## Skill Tracker uchun tavsiya

Har bir subskillga quyidagi maydonlarni biriktirish mumkin:

- Priority: Core, Important, Optional
- Target Level: Foundation, Junior, Middle, Strong Middle, Senior, Lead
- Status: Not Started, Learning, Practicing, Proficient, Expert
- Practical Task: bilimni amalda tekshirish topshirig‘i
- Acceptance Criteria: skillni egallagan deb hisoblash mezonlari
- Prerequisites: oldindan bilish kerak bo‘lgan skilllar

Muhim jihat: barcha subskilllarni Senior darajasida chuqur bilish shart emas. Masalan, AWS, RabbitMQ va Microservices ko‘p loyihalarda foydali, lekin ularning zarurligi vakansiya va kompaniya arxitekturasiga bog‘liq. PHP, Laravel, SQL, ma’lumotlar bazasi optimizatsiyasi, API, testing, debugging, security va system design esa Senior Laravel yo‘nalishining markaziy ko‘nikmalaridir.
